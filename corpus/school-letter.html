HILLSIDE ELEMENTARY SCHOOL FAMILY BULLETIN

SEPTEMBER FAMILY BULLETIN. PLEASE READ THE WHOLE DOCUMENT AND RETURN THE SIGNATURE PAGE BY THE DATE GIVEN BELOW.

Announcements

Dates to remember

Bus route changes take effect on the first Monday of the month. Routes 4 and 11 are combined, and the new pickup time for the Ridge Road stop is fourteen minutes earlier than last year. Families on Ridge Road should plan for the earlier time from the first day, because the driver will not wait past the posted minute.

Picture day is the second Tuesday of the month. Order envelopes go home in backpacks one week before, and the photographer does not accept late orders.

The cafeteria is moving to a four week menu cycle. Allergy substitutions continue as before, but the kitchen now needs the substitution form on file before the first day of each cycle rather than each week, so a form filed once in September covers the rest of the term.

Parent teacher conferences run over three afternoons in the last full week of the month. Sign up sheets are at the front desk and fill quickly.

After school clubs open for registration at the end of the second week. Chess, robotics, and choir have capacity limits, and last year every one of them filled on the first afternoon. Registration is in person at the office, and the office cannot hold a place over the telephone.

The signature page for this bulletin is due back on the last school day of the month. A missing signature page means your child cannot ride the activity bus.

If you need help reading this letter

Call the school office and ask for the family liaison. Someone can read the bulletin to you over the telephone, in English or in Spanish, and a large print copy can be printed while you wait. The office is open from half past seven in the morning until four in the afternoon on school days.

This bulletin is also posted on the school notice board in the front hallway. Read the district calendar.
