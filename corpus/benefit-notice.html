NOTICE OF ACTION ON YOUR BENEFITS

What we decided

What you have to do

Your household benefit amount changes beginning the first day of next month. The new monthly amount is lower than the amount you receive now because the income reported on your last review is higher than the income used for the current amount. This notice explains the change and what you can do about it.

You do not have to do anything for the new amount to start. If you think the new amount is wrong, you can ask for a fair hearing, and you can ask for one in person, by telephone, or in writing.

The change applies to every person listed on the case. Nobody is being removed from the case by this notice, and your medical coverage is not affected by this notice.

If you ask for a hearing before the effective date, your benefits stay at the current amount until the hearing decision. If you lose the hearing, you may have to pay back the difference.

How to ask for a hearing

Call the number on the back of your benefits card, or write to the county office at the address printed at the bottom of this page. Tell us the case number, the date on this notice, and why you think the decision is wrong. Keep a copy of anything you send. If you need help, a free legal aid office can help you prepare, and an interpreter is available at no cost.
