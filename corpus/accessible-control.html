Riverton Public Library will open on Sunday afternoons

Starting on the first Sunday of next month, the library opens from one in the afternoon until five in the afternoon. The change adds four hours a week and does not remove any hours from the current schedule.

Getting help on a Sunday

One librarian and one assistant are on duty on Sunday afternoons. The computer room, the printer, and the meeting room are all open, and the meeting room can be booked at the desk on the day.

If you need a large print copy of this notice, or a copy read to you over the telephone, call the desk during opening hours and ask for the duty librarian.
